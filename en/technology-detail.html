Technology & architecture

KAP technology and architecture

By combining advanced CT sensors, custom hardware and AI software, KAP delivers an integrated solution for smart power-grid management.

Dedicated sensor

KAP dedicated CT sensor

KAP's dedicated CT (current transformer) sensors, with a unique design, can disaggregate the load of each household appliance — with no separate sensor on each one. The sensors are installed at the device and identify each appliance's consumption pattern with high accuracy.

Hardware integration

Integration with smart meters

KAP's technology is designed to work alongside existing smart meters — with no change to the distribution network's infrastructure. Working with meter manufacturers, KAP modules connect plug-and-play and start collecting and analysing data straight away.

  • ✅ Compatible with single- and three-phase installations
  • ✅ Installed without interrupting customers' supply
  • ✅ Supports DLMS/COSEM protocols
  • ✅ NB-IoT and LoRa wireless connectivity
  • ✅ Over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates
Fault detection

Fault detection within a 10 m radius

Using deep-learning algorithms, KAP's fault-detection system can instantly identify distribution-network faults — short circuits, current leakage, phase imbalance and equipment failure — within 10 m of where the ESA is installed. Instant alerts are sent to network operators via the app and SMS.
