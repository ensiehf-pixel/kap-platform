FAQ — industry

Is KAP going to switch off factory equipment?

No; that isn't the basis of the product. In industry, control must respect process constraints, critical loads, continuous production and operating permits.

How does KAP help when power is restricted?

The aim can be to better understand which loads can be managed and to evaluate scenarios for reducing or shifting load, so decisions aren't just guesswork.

Can KAP prevent blackouts?

KAP cannot guarantee preventing grid blackouts. But better consumption and load management can support demand-side decisions.

Does KAP detect current leakage and safety hazards?

By analysing unusual consumption patterns and sudden load changes, KAP can give early warning of possible insulation problems, overloads and current leakage. It does not replace residual-current devices (RCDs); it is a complementary layer of smart monitoring.

Why do factory electricity costs go up?

In industry, auxiliary equipment left running outside production hours, heavy loads starting at the same time and exceeding the contracted capacity all drive up costs. In the hot season, grid restrictions can also disrupt production. Without measuring each line and machine, you can't tell which load can be shifted.

How is KAP's ESA installed, and how long does it take?

Installation starts with a short technical visit: the circuit map, measurement points and list of controllable loads are recorded. Then split-core current sensors (CT) are clamped onto the panel cables with no power cut and no rewiring, and the ESA is mounted on the wall next to the panel. Installation time depends on the number of panels and circuits and is confirmed for each site after the visit.

KAP in practice: from installation to results in your factory or workshop

KAP works in five steps, and each step has a clear output that operators and the technical manager can see.

  1. Scoping and site visit: decide which panels and circuits are monitored and which loads must never be controlled.
  2. Baseline: current consumption, past bills, working days and weather are recorded so a fair comparison is possible later.
  3. Understanding consumption: The ESA separates the consumption patterns of auxiliary equipment, cooling, hall lighting and shiftable loads, and shows wasted use.
  4. Alerts and safe control: an alert and a chance to fix it come first; if wasteful use continues, only pre-approved loads are controlled and every command is logged with its time and reason.
  5. Results report: energy saved, peak demand and contracted-capacity status are reported against the baseline so the next decision is based on real numbers.

Lower consumption lasts when users understand the reason for each recommendation and see the result; that's why KAP treats short training, simple reports and the ability to log exceptions as part of the service.

What are the results?

KAP measures each line and machine separately, identifies loads that can be shifted without affecting production and, by controlling when approved loads run together, keeps demand within the contracted capacity. Continuous and sensitive processes are assessed technically on their own before being brought into the control scope. Results are measured the same way as in the University of Tehran pilot, following the IPMVP framework.

What are the costs and terms?

Deployment and installation of KAP are completely free; in return, an energy-saving certificate contract is signed with the customer. The contract is based on verified savings, measured against the baseline following the IPMVP framework. The installation scope and contract details are set for each site after a technical visit. KAP does not promise the same savings to every building, because results depend on each site's conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Will the production line stop?

No. Only pre-approved loads are controlled, and continuous and sensitive processes are brought into scope only after a separate technical assessment and with the operator's consent.

Is KAP suitable for small workshops too?

Yes. Small workshops with identifiable loads and manageable processes are among the first industrial areas KAP serves.

How are generator load and grid load separated?

KAP measures and reports load supplied by the grid and by the generator separately, so the benefit of each is calculated correctly.

Does KAP also detect equipment faults or overloads?

Consumption-pattern analysis can reveal unusual behaviour and overloads earlier and send alerts; accuracy depends on the type of load, sensor location and data quality.

Will our production data stay confidential?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is tiered, and data rights are set out in the contract.
