Test version and operational pilot

What stage is KAP at today?

A technology platform in artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things (IoT), energy data analytics, smart load management and the digital energy economy, building a new generation of infrastructure for managing and trading electrical energy.

Beyond conventional BMS and monitoring

Taking an innovative approach beyond conventional building-management systems (BMS) and simple energy-monitoring systems, this project aims to create a smart platform for identifying, analysing, controlling and ultimately Making the value of electrical energy tradable .

Capability status

What does the test version do today?

  • Consumption monitoring
  • Data transfer and storage
  • Consumption-pattern analysis
  • Identifying opportunities worth reviewing
  • Reports and dashboard
  • Developing alerts and recommendations

What is still in development?

  • Wide-scale automatic control
  • Full disaggregation of all loads
  • Mass deployment
  • Full connection to savings certificates
  • VPP at national scale

Early pilot results

Lower consumption and demand management

In the early pilot, the project management has reported a reduction in the demand index compared with the baseline.

The University of Tehran pilot has been carried out, and we are now looking for other faculties and universities to deploy it.

⚠️ This result does not guarantee performance at every site; developing the measurement and validation method is part of the project's current path.
