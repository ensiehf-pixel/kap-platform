Introducing KAP's new generation of dedicated CT sensors for the Smart Energy Monitoring Device (SEMD)

📅 24 August 2026 ⏱ 6 min read ✍️ KAP engineering team

Load disaggregation is one of the most important capabilities of the new generation of smart meters. KAP's dedicated CT sensors, with an innovative design, can identify the usage pattern of each household appliance without installing a separate sensor on each one.

Conventional CT sensors vs. KAP's dedicated CT sensors

Conventional CT sensors measure only the total current. KAP's sensors, sampling at 50 kHz on 16 simultaneous channels, can recognise the unique electrical signature of each appliance.

Every electrical appliance has a unique electrical 'fingerprint'. KAP's dedicated CT sensors identify and track that fingerprint.

Technical specifications

• Measurement accuracy: ±0.5% (class 0.5)
• 16 simultaneous measurement channels
• Sampling rate: 50 kHz
• Current range: 0.1 to 100 A
• Output: digital (SPI/I2C)
• Service life: 10+ years
• Protection: IP54

Installation and operating benefits

The biggest advantage of KAP's dedicated CT sensors is that separate smart plugs are no longer needed. Instead of dozens of smart plugs in every home, a single CT sensor is installed at the main meter and disaggregates the load of every appliance. This cuts installation cost by up to 80% and makes maintenance much simpler.
