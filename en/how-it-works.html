How it works and the architecture

From consumption data to tangible results

KAP doesn't just display consumption. The goal is to turn raw energy data into deep insight into opportunities, operational decisions and provable savings.

KAP's 5-step cycle

1

Real-time monitoring

Electricity use at key points is measured without cutting power, using clamp sensors or a connection to digital smart meters. Data is recorded securely in real time and sent to the cloud.

2

Pattern and behaviour analysis

KAP's AI engine processes consumption behaviour during working hours, grid peaks and closures. Hidden losses, standby loads and voltage fluctuations are separated out and revealed.

3

Identifying load-management opportunities

The system pinpoints bottlenecks that can be optimised without harming comfort or production — from rescheduling pumps and chillers to moving energy-heavy processes to off-peak hours.

4

Action that keeps the user in control

Practical recommendations tailored to each site are provided. KAP never makes unwanted interventions or forced cuts; the decision stays with the manager or resident.

5

Measuring results against the baseline

The effect of every action is evaluated against the consumption baseline and weather variables, so every rial of financial savings and every reduction in peak load is documented and provable.
