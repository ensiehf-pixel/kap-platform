About us

KAP started with one question: how can electricity data become a decision that actually delivers results?

A technology platform in artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things (IoT), energy data analytics, smart load management and the digital energy economy, building a new generation of infrastructure for managing and trading electrical energy.

Going beyond conventional building management systems (BMS) and monitoring-only tools, KAP is building a smart foundation to identify, analyse and control electrical energy — and, in time, to make its value tradable.

An electricity bill shows past use; real management needs to know where the opportunity is, make the right decision and measure the result precisely. KAP works in smart demand-side management, energy monitoring and consumption analytics, turning usage data into actionable steps and measurable results.

KAP began with educational and university buildings: the KAP pilot has been carried out, and we are now looking for other faculties and universities to deploy it. After that, development extends to residential, office, commercial and industrial sites. In the long run, KAP aims to link demand-management capacity into a national virtual power plant (VPP) and make verified savings economically usable.

In plain words

KAP helps buildings see where and why they use electricity, and cut waste with alerts and safe control. What you get is not just a chart — it is a clear action and a result you can measure.

KAP's mission

Mission KAP (Kara Energy Pardazan Hooshmand) is to cut unnecessary consumption and manage power demand while keeping building services at full quality. Monitoring the building's supply, analysing usage patterns, controlling permitted loads and reporting the result form one chain: Measure → Analyse → Act → Verify → Monetise. Selling a device is not the goal; the goal is a result the customer can see and measure.

What KAP works on

At KAP's core is electricity demand management and an ongoing relationship with the customer. Alongside it, KAP works in smart monitoring and control equipment, thermal energy storage and EV charging infrastructure , each with its own accounts and contracts so every activity's result stays clear.

What does KAP build?

KAP's solution is one connected chain: current sensors (CT) measure each circuit without cutting power, a controller enforces safety rules locally, even when the internet is down, an analytics engine identifies appliance usage patterns with load disaggregation (NILM), and an app with three access levels (user, engineer, management) turns the result into alerts and actionable steps.

Where is KAP's pilot?

KAP's pilot runs at the Faculty of World Studies, University of Tehran has been carried out, and we are now looking for other faculties and universities to deploy it; after that, development continues to other organisational customers. KAP is pursuing placement in the University of Tehran's incubator and Science & Technology Park.

How is the result measured?

KAP reports results against an adjusted baseline — one that accounts for working days, weather and changes of use, so savings caused by a holiday are never credited to the system. The method follows the international framework IPMVP , and each building's energy (kWh), demand (kW) and contracted capacity are reported separately.

KAP's business lines

KAP works along three lines: creating and verifying rights from energy savings, selling and installing smart monitoring and control equipment, and developing cable EV charging using the capacity it frees up — alongside monitoring, installation and support services.
