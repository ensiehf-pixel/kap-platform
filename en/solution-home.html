Why is my home electricity bill so high?

The bill only shows total consumption; it doesn't say which appliance used how much electricity and when. Appliances left on, standby use, poorly set air conditioners or heaters and heavy appliances running together at peak hours are common causes — but their share differs in every home, and without measuring you can't tell which matters.

How is KAP's ESA installed, and how long does it take?

Installation starts with a short technical visit: the circuit map, measurement points and list of controllable loads are recorded. Then split-core current sensors (CT) are clamped onto the panel cables with no power cut and no rewiring, and the ESA is mounted on the wall next to the panel. Installation time depends on the number of panels and circuits and is confirmed for each site after the visit.

KAP in practice: from installation to results in your home

KAP works in five steps, and each step has a clear output the whole family can see.

  1. Scoping and site visit: decide which panels and circuits are monitored and which loads must never be controlled.
  2. Baseline: current consumption, past bills, working days and weather are recorded so a fair comparison is possible later.
  3. Understanding consumption: The ESA separates the consumption patterns of appliances such as water heaters, air conditioners, electric heaters and chargers, and shows wasted use.
  4. Alerts and safe control: an alert and a chance to fix it come first; if wasteful use continues, only pre-approved loads are controlled and every command is logged with its time and reason.
  5. Results report: energy saved, peak demand and contracted-capacity status are reported against the baseline so the next decision is based on real numbers.

Lower consumption lasts when users understand the reason for each recommendation and see the result; that's why KAP treats short training, simple reports and the ability to log exceptions as part of the service.

What are the results?

In the app you see where and when your home uses electricity, you get alerts about unnecessary use, and only the appliances you have approved are controlled smartly; the fridge, medical equipment and essential appliances are never switched off. The method is the same one used in KAP's University of Tehran pilot: alert first, a chance to fix it, and only then approved control.

What are the costs and terms?

Pricing is proposed after the site visit, based on the scope of installation and service. A contract usually includes an installation and setup fee and a recurring monitoring and support fee; equipment can be bought or provided as a service. Sharing in the savings is also possible, provided the baseline and calculation method are clearly agreed. KAP does not promise the same savings to every building, because results depend on each site's conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Will KAP's ESA switch off my home appliances without permission?

No. Control is enabled only for appliances you have approved in advance, and you always get an alert first. Essential appliances are on a separate list and are never controlled.

Do I need to rewire my home for installation?

No. Split-core current sensors are clamped onto the panel cables without cutting the power; there's no need to open walls or change wiring.

How is KAP different from my home electricity meter?

The distribution company's meter records your home's total consumption for billing. KAP's ESA sees each circuit's consumption and appliance patterns and turns them into alerts and action.

Is my home's consumption data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is tiered, and it is never shared with anyone else without your permission.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The ESA has local processing, so safety and control rules keep running inside your home even when the internet is down.
